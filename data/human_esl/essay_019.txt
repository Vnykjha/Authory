# question: In many countries, people now wear western clothes such as suits and jeans rather than traditional clothing. Why is this the case? Is this a positive or negative development?
# overall_band: 5.5
# label: human
# category: human_esl

Today, everything is evolving, so the fashion trends of people are constantly being updated and changed. Traditional clothes are increasingly being overshadowed by Western clothes. In my opinion, this is quite a negative development.

There are many reasons for this trend. One of the most obvious reasons for the development of this trend is the growth and development of technology and communication. In fact, the media has provided designers with the trends of the times pages that people liked at that time. Moreover, the mass media or online newspapers often have articles that help people recognize special aspects and characteristics of Western style, in order to help them to know fashion trends at the moment to change. In addition, some people believe that nowadays everything has evolved, therefore, the perspective of fashion trends and how to dress is also noticed by everyone. People wearing traditional clothes are often considered people who do not keep up with trends and are despised when going out in society. In fact, today, many traditional costumes are designed in a complicated way, with many motifs and people have a lot of trouble wearing them. In contrast, Western clothes bring coolness and comfort to consumers. The above reasons have explained why today traditional costumes are being used less and less.

However, changing fashion styles as well as increasingly forgetting the existence of traditional clothes will lose the cultural identity of that nation. For each country, the determination of sovereignty, territory and people is through the traditions and culture of that country. So, if everyone goes out wearing Western clothes, there will be a lot of countries that lose part of their culture, which is the custom of fashion. In addition, in some places, some weather would not be suitable for people to wear western clothes. It can be seen that this is a trend that does not bring too many positive things to countries around the world.

Summing up, we gradually prefer western fashion because of its more youthful and cool characteristics. In addition, this is a trend that is not too positive for countries around the world.
