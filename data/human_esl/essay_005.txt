# question: In many countries, people now wear western clothes such as suits and jeans rather than traditional clothing. Why is this the case? Is this a positive or negative development?

Nowadays many people are influenced by Western outfits like jeans and suits. They enjoy wearing them instead of traditional attires. This trend can be caused by several reasons and I believe that its impact is positive.

Several factors affect choosing these types of clothing. Firstly, jeans and suits are more comfortable than traditional apparel. Today people seek comfort and freedom, so they prefer them.  Secondly, they look more fashionable. It cannot be denied that the West is the heart of fashion and plenty of trends come from there. You can even see a wide variety of casual clothes like jeans and T-shirts everywhere. Therefore, more people have access to them and they take every opportunity to put on those clothes to look more stylish.

As for potential implications, I believe that this tendency has certain positive effects. Initially, western outfits give people more choices, as they can wear one piece of apparel to many places. They are more functional. However, traditional clothes are not suitable for everywhere. It would be appropriate to wear them to national occasions such as festivals. Moreover, this kind of wear allows people to express themselves easily. It is hard to do with national outfits. I think it is interesting to see how individuals use clothes as a form of expression. Many people like to wear different outfits and show off their personality.

In conclusion, I think the main causes of this matter are comfort and fashion. Besides, this trend is advantageous in my own opinion.
