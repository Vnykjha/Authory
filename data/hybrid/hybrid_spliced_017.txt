In the present day, the way people dress has changed a lot compared to before. However, not so that the traditional costumes are forgotten, some are still used in the traditional holidays. On the other hand, it is really difficult to maintain the same way of dressing as before. This essay will discuss why this problem happening and indicate whether it is a positive or negative issue.

There are several reasons why books and the written word are not going to be replaced by the internet. First of all, books are portable. You can take them with you wherever you go. You can read them on the bus, on the train, or in a park. You can even read them in bed. The internet is not portable. You can only use it if you have a computer or a smartphone with an internet connection. Second, books and the written word are more permanent than the internet. Once a book is published, it is not going to change. The internet is constantly changing. Information that is available today may not be available tomorrow. Third, books and the written word are more reliable than the internet. It is easy to find inaccurate information on the internet. Fourth, books and the written word are more private than the internet. When you are reading a book, no one knows what you are reading. When you are using the internet, people can see what you are doing.

Although we know that today's society uses new clothes, it brings many negative effects to traditional clothes. It is inevitable that later generations will forget the costumes created by their ancestors. This leads us to lose our country's traditions. For example, the Ao Dai, a typical Vietnamese costume, has existed for a long time and until today, there have been many new variations. However, this can destroy the morale that the costume brings to the people.

In conclusion, people use fashionable outfits because of their popularity. On the contrary, this has negatively affected the costume culture from the past to the present. That's why I think it is still a good idea to preserve traditional clothes to pass on to future generations.
